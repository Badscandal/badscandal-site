About the studio

I'm Luke. BADSCANDAL started as the name on my records and turned into the room where everything gets made — the music, the films around the music, and the web experiences around the films.

The idea has never changed: make the music you actually mean, say the things you're not supposed to, and stop performing for people who were never really listening.

What it's all about

Badscandal is honest, progressive and forward-thinking — anarchy of the personal kind. The world you live in exists in your mind as an idea, and most people just accept the version they were handed. This project is about refusing that: generating your own version of the world, decided on deliberately — not living as a drone on autopilot, fed information, told what to do, afraid of being seen.

The opinions of others are valueless. Judgement — and the fear of it — is biological wiring, not something that deserves your genuine attention. The average life is about four thousand weeks. Badscandal is about spending yours expressing who you are.

Everyone is welcome here — every race, every sex, every orientation. The goal is to supersede offence, outgrow the fear of judgement, and live honestly from a place of not giving a f***.

The name

Badscandal isn't an alias — it's a brand. It's the world the music lives in: one name across the records, the visuals and the shows. As the project grows and tours, the tour is Badscandal. Everything we do is a Badscandal.

The whole thing comes at the world from one angle — not giving a f***. The name is for the people who cared too much, got hurt doing it, and came out the other side realising nothing really matters except the moment you're standing in. It's a place with no judgement, where we push past what's considered normal — in life and in music. Everything here is designed to shock. So let's shock together. Join the movement.

Principles

Mean it or bin it
If a song, a frame or a page doesn't say something true, it doesn't ship. Taste is the second filter. Honesty is the first.
All in or nothing
Fewer projects, everything given. When BADSCANDAL takes something on, it gets the whole obsession, not a slice of it.
Human first, always
Real players, real rooms, real texture. Whatever tools get used, they're never the point — the feeling is.
Trouble over polish
Rushed work is forgettable and over-polished work is worse. The edge stays in. The mistakes that mean something stay in.

How it goes

  1. Step 01

    Hearts first

    We're not an agency and we don't act like one. Badscandal is a collective that cares about exactly one thing: pouring genuine feeling into the work until it means something. If it doesn't move us, it doesn't leave the room.

  2. Step 02

    We make the thing

    Recorded live where it counts, designed and cut in the same room, graded until every shot sits in the same world and nothing feels out of place.

  3. Step 03

    We let it loose

    It ships, it misbehaves, it gets talked about. Then we watch what the world does with it and go again.

What you should know.

What does BADSCANDAL stand for?

No kings, no gods, no masters. Badscandal stands for anarchy of the personal kind — expressing yourself freely and being yourself, whatever that means for you. It means being the badscandal: not a puppet, not a people-pleaser, not a version of yourself built for someone else's comfort.

Is there merch I can wear to show my support?

Of course. The first drop is being built right now — get through the door first at the store.

What if you receive complaints about content, or offend someone?

We don't care — we're here to express. If everything you make is safe for everyone, it says nothing to anyone.
