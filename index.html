About BADSCANDAL

We left. Country, family, friends, the whole set-up — traded for a dream of making things and seeing the world. We met, fell for each other embarrassingly fast, and decided life was too short to do it the sensible way.

BADSCANDAL is the clothing that came out of that. Say the thing nobody says. Chase the thing you actually want, out loud, in front of everybody.

Four thousand weeks

That's the whole allocation. If you're thirty you've spent about fifteen hundred of them already. Nobody mentions this, because a person who has done the arithmetic is much harder to keep in line.

Everyone will have something to say. Too fast, too crazy, too much, not normal. They said it before you and they'll say it after you, and none of it will matter — nobody studies Shakespeare's private life. The work outlived the man and the gossip didn't survive the century. Yours won't either.

Society is built to keep things running smoothly, not to get you where you want to go. Work, rent, repeat — and most people never get out the door. If you're not breaking the law, there's no problem. There was never a problem.

The name

BADSCANDAL is about becoming one. A bad scandal is what they call you when you stop asking. It's for the people who cared too much, got hurt doing it, and came out the other side realising nothing really matters except the moment you're standing in.

Everyone is welcome here — every race, every sex, every orientation. The goal is to outgrow the fear of being judged and live honestly from a place of not giving a f***. The clothes are the statement. Follow us around the world.

Principles

Mean it or bin it
If a piece doesn't say something true, it doesn't go in the drop. Taste is the second filter. Honesty is the first.
All in or nothing
Fewer pieces, everything given. We'd rather make ten things we'd wear every day than a hundred we wouldn't.
Say it out loud
If a statement needs explaining, it wasn't a statement. It goes across the back where people can read it from behind you.
Trouble over polish
Rushed work is forgettable and over-polished work is worse. The edge stays in.

How it goes

  1. Step 01

    We say it first

    Every piece starts as something one of us actually said out loud and then thought better of. Those are the good ones. If it doesn't make us slightly nervous, it doesn't get printed.

  2. Step 02

    We make the thing

    Heavyweight cotton, printed big enough to read from across the road. Most of the statements live across the back, because that's the side of you people are looking at when you walk away.

  3. Step 03

    You wear it out

    Somebody reads it, somebody has an opinion about it, and that's the entire point. Then we go again.

What you should know.

What does BADSCANDAL stand for?

Being the bad scandal. Anarchy of the personal kind — expressing yourself freely and being yourself, whatever that means for you. Not a puppet, not a people-pleaser, not a version of yourself built for someone else's comfort.

Who's behind it?

Luke and Lilian. We left our country to chase making things and seeing the world, and started a clothing brand as a statement to ourselves that we were actually doing it. The whole story is here.

What's the difference between Statements and Essentials?

Statements have something to say across them — that's the loud half of the wardrobe. Essentials are the blanks and basics you build the rest of the outfit from. Both are in the store.

What if someone's offended by what's printed on it?

We don't care — we're here to express. If everything you make is safe for everyone, it says nothing to anyone. If you're not breaking the law, there's no problem.

Where do you ship?

Worldwide. Prices are in euro, and everything is made to order — so give it a little longer than you'd give a warehouse.
