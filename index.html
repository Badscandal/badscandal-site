About BADSCANDAL

I'm Luke. BADSCANDAL started as something my sister called me and turned into a brand you can put on — clothing for the people who never quite fit the room they were standing in.

The idea has never changed: make things you actually mean, say the things you're not supposed to, and stop performing for people who were never really watching.

What it's all about

Badscandal is honest, progressive and forward-thinking — anarchy of the personal kind. The world you live in exists in your mind as an idea, and most people just accept the version they were handed. This project is about refusing that: generating your own version of the world, decided on deliberately — not living as a drone on autopilot, fed information, told what to do, afraid of being seen.

The opinions of others are valueless. Judgement — and the fear of it — is biological wiring, not something that deserves your genuine attention. The average life is about four thousand weeks. Badscandal is about spending yours expressing who you are.

Everyone is welcome here — every race, every sex, every orientation. The goal is to supersede offence, outgrow the fear of judgement, and live honestly from a place of not giving a f***.

The name

Badscandal isn't an alias — it's a brand. One name across the clothing, the visuals and the shows. What you wear is the loudest thing about you before you open your mouth, so we put it on the front of a shirt. Everything we do is a Badscandal.

The whole thing comes at the world from one angle — not giving a f***. The name is for the people who cared too much, got hurt doing it, and came out the other side realising nothing really matters except the moment you're standing in. It's a place with no judgement, where we push past what's considered normal — in life and on your back. Everything here is designed to shock. So let's shock together. Join the movement.

Principles

Mean it or bin it
If a print, a fit or a page doesn't say something true, it doesn't ship. Taste is the second filter. Honesty is the first.
All in or nothing
Fewer pieces, everything given. When BADSCANDAL makes something, it gets the whole obsession, not a slice of it.
Human first, always
Real weight, real fabric, real texture. Whatever it costs to make it properly, that's what it costs — the feeling is the point.
Trouble over polish
Rushed work is forgettable and over-polished work is worse. The edge stays in. The mistakes that mean something stay in.

How it goes

  1. Step 01

    Hearts first

    We're not a clothing brand chasing a trend and we don't act like one. Badscandal cares about exactly one thing: pouring genuine feeling into the work until it means something. If it doesn't move us, it doesn't leave the room.

  2. Step 02

    We make the thing

    Drawn, printed, worn, argued over and cut again — until the weight is right, the fit is right, and the thing says exactly what it's supposed to say from across a room.

  3. Step 03

    We let it loose

    It ships, it misbehaves, it gets talked about. Then we watch what the world does with it and go again.

What you should know.

What does BADSCANDAL stand for?

No kings, no gods, no masters. Badscandal stands for anarchy of the personal kind — expressing yourself freely and being yourself, whatever that means for you. It means being the badscandal: not a puppet, not a people-pleaser, not a version of yourself built for someone else's comfort.

What do you actually sell?

T-shirts and hoodies. Heavyweight, properly made, printed with the things most brands wouldn't put their name near. Men's and women's cuts — it's all in the store.

How do I know what size to get?

Every product has a size guide on it — real measurements, not vibes. If you're between sizes and you want it boxy, size up. Still stuck? Ask us.

What if you offend someone?

We don't care — we're here to express. If everything you make is safe for everyone, it says nothing to anyone.

Are there any live shows?

Yes, there are indeed. Head to Live shows — dates land there first.
