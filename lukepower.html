Info

Luke Power is a musician, artist and multi-instrumentalist from Dublin, Ireland — a producer and mixing engineer who has worked with a long list of high-profile artists and built a serious name for himself behind the desk. Renowned for his mixes, his work has racked up over 2 billion streams across the records he's touched. For inquiries, reach out at contact@badscandal.com.

The story

Luke didn't really choose music — he was just never without it. A natural musician with perfect pitch, he could pick up almost any instrument and play it. I don't remember ever not doing music, he says. Everything else came harder. Home was distant and short on warmth, and he spent his childhood feeling like the odd one out — too emotional for the people around him, too strange for the room.

The people who changed that were his grandparents, Helen and Liam Power, who took him in at twelve. I think they're the reason I'm not a monster, he says. I didn't feel like I had to survive with them — I felt like I was living. Losing them both, slowly and then suddenly, marked him for good; a lot of who he is now traces straight back to them.

The name came from his sister, Elise, who told him plainly that he was scandalous — that the way he thought, spoke and made things never quite lined up with how everyone else did it. It stuck. I don't give a f*** what anyone thinks, he says. I did as a kid. Not anymore.

That's the whole project in a line. Badscandal is for the people who never quite fit — who got tired of trying, tired of being managed and manipulated, tired of pretending. It's music made from a different angle: unfiltered, uncensored, no marketing voice, nothing played safe for radio. We're all going to die one day — that's the one thing we're all definitely doing together. So don't let fear stop you. Open up, say what you want, push the boundary. That's the whole thing.
