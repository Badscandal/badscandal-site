Activate in your browser · for computers that cannot reach the licence server

  1. In the plug-in, click Can't connect? Activate in your browser, then COPY next to the machine code.
  2. Enter your licence key and paste the machine code here, then click Get licence.
  3. Copy the licence text this page gives you, paste it into the plug-in's licence field and click APPLY.

Paste it into the plug-in and click APPLY. This used one of your seats, exactly like a normal activation.

The key came in your purchase email from keys@mail.badscandal.com. Lost it? Email contact@badscandal.com. Each key works on two computers; deactivate one from the plug-in's footer to free a seat.
